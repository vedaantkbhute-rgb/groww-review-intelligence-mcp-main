## 📝 Appended Entry: 2026-10-11 (Approved by: Product Manager)

### Identified Fee Issue:
Mutual Fund Exit Load Misunderstanding (Unexpected 1% Deduction)

### Top Themes:
- **Pricing & Unexpected Exit Load Charges**: Users report surprise deductions (1% exit load) when redeeming equity mutual funds before 365 days without in-app warning.
- **SIP Mandate & Payment Processing Delays**: Complaints regarding Auto-pay failures, delayed unit allotment, and BSE Star MF mandate glitches.
- **App Experience & Portfolio Tracking**: Positive feedback on dark mode UI, fund search, and requests for improved annual capital gains filters.

### Weekly Product Pulse:
```
WEEKLY PRODUCT PULSE — MUTUAL FUNDS
Date Range: 2026-08-01 to 2026-10-10 | Volume: 25 Reviews

SUMMARY OF TOP THEMES:
Customer feedback is dominated by: Pricing & Unexpected Exit Load Charges (56.0%), SIP Mandate & Payment Processing Delays (16.0%), App Experience & Portfolio Tracking (16.0%).

SUPPORTING USER QUOTES:
• “I redeemed my HDFC Flexi Cap fund units after 8 months and noticed almost 1% was deducted as some fee called 'Exit Load'! Why was this not clearly shown on the sell screen?” — Aditya Sharma
• “Hidden charges everywhere. When I withdrew my money after 5 months, an exit load fee of ₹1,450 was cut from my balance. Nobody warned me during redemption that 1 year wasn't completed.” — Pooja Hegde

KEY OBSERVATION:
Retail investors heavily misunderstand mutual fund Exit Loads. Over 40% of negative reviews mistake the AMC's statutory 1% early redemption load for hidden broker commission. Users prematurely sell units days before the 365-day cutoff without realizing penalties apply.

3 PRODUCT ACTION IDEAS:
1. Add an 'Exit Load Countdown Pill' on the sell screen displaying exact days remaining until 0% penalty.
2. Integrate real-time 'Estimated Net Payout' breakdown before T-PIN authorization so deductions are never a surprise.
3. Add proactive portfolio warning banners: 'Hold for X more days to save 1% exit load' on near-cutoff holdings.
```

### Fee Explanation (Customer Support Snippet):
- Exit Load is a statutory fee levied by the Asset Management Company (AMC)—not Groww—to discourage premature redemptions and protect long-term investors.
- For standard equity mutual funds (e.g. HDFC Flexi Cap), an exit load of 1.00% applies if units are redeemed or switched within 365 days (1 year) from the allotment date.
- If fund units are redeemed after completing 365 days from the allotment date, the applicable exit load is strictly Nil (0%).
- Switching between schemes or moving from Regular to Direct plans is legally treated as a redemption and fresh purchase, attracting exit loads if held under 365 days.
- All exit load proceeds are credited directly back into the mutual fund scheme portfolio for the benefit of continuing unit holders, as mandated by SEBI guidelines.
- Official sources: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/knowledge-center/exit-load.html and https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/jun-2023/master-circular-for-mutual-funds_73229.html. Last checked: 2026-10-02.

### Verified Sources:
- [AMFI India - Mutual Fund Exit Loads Explained](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/knowledge-center/exit-load.html)
- [SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds (Categorization & Rationalization)](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/jun-2023/master-circular-for-mutual-funds_73229.html)
