### Standardized Customer-Facing Explanation (<= 6 Bullets)
* **What is an Exit Load?**  
  Exit Load is a statutory fee levied directly by the Asset Management Company (AMC)—not Groww—to discourage premature redemptions and protect long-term fund performance for all unitholders.
* **Standard Rate for Equity Schemes:**  
  For most open-ended equity schemes (such as HDFC Flexi Cap or Large Cap), an exit load of **1.00%** applies if fund units are redeemed or switched within **365 days (1 year)** from the date of allotment.
* **Zero Exit Load After 1 Year:**  
  If fund units are redeemed after completing 365 days from the date of allotment, the applicable exit load is strictly **Nil (0.00%)**.
* **Switching Between Schemes:**  
  Switching from one scheme to another or transitioning from Regular to Direct plans is legally classified as a redemption followed by a fresh purchase, attracting the 1% exit load if held under 365 days.
* **Where Do the Exit Load Proceeds Go?**  
  In compliance with SEBI regulations, all exit load deductions are credited directly back into the mutual fund scheme portfolio for the benefit of continuing investors, rather than retained by Groww or the AMC.
* **Official Regulatory Source Links:**  
  Refer to the [AMFI Investor Knowledge Center on Exit Loads](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/knowledge-center/exit-load.html) and the [SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/jun-2023/master-circular-for-mutual-funds_73229.html).
